SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (~20s)
Every family has a shoebox of fading photographs. Memories that grow quieter every year. Pixelyaad is an AI Photo Memory Vault that brings them back to life. Built for the Cloudinary AI Hackathon, Track one: AI Media Pipelines. Upload an old photo, and watch artificial intelligence restore it, describe it, and keep it searchable forever.

SEGMENT 2 — UPLOAD (~40s)
Let's start with an old, faded family photograph. I drag it into Pixelyaad, and the upload begins immediately. Here's what makes this special: the photo travels straight from the browser to Cloudinary using an unsigned upload preset. No A P I keys, no secrets in the code, no server in the middle. You can watch the progress bar fill as the bytes stream up. This architecture is deliberate. It keeps credentials out of the repository entirely, which means the project is safe to open source, and it scales effortlessly because uploads never touch our backend.

SEGMENT 3 — AI TAGS (~40s)
The moment the upload finishes, Cloudinary's Google Auto Tagging analyzes the image. Look at the response: family, vintage clothing, child, suit, dress, family reunion. Real, meaningful tags, generated by A I, returned right inside the upload response. Pixelyaad saves every tag with the photo, and then goes one step further. It drafts a caption from those tags automatically, in English and in Hindi, which you can edit before saving. The photo is now part of your vault, fully described and ready to be found again.

SEGMENT 4 — RESTORE (~45s)
Now the magic. This photograph is decades old: faded, scratched, washed out. I open it and drag the restore slider. On the left, the original. On the right, the same photo after Cloudinary's generative restore. Scratches gone, contrast rebuilt, faces clear again. This is the e gen restore transformation, applied as a simple U R L parameter, no machine learning code on our side at all. And with one toggle, e improve enhances sharpness and color even further. Before, and after. A memory, rescued. For a family photo like this, that single slider is the whole demo.

SEGMENT 5 — MEMORY CARD (~35s)
Pixelyaad doesn't just store photos, it celebrates them. With one click, it generates a memory card: a tall, shareable portrait with the caption set in gold over the image. And this card isn't rendered in the browser. It's a Cloudinary transformation U R L, with a text overlay baked in on the fly. Share the link anywhere and it looks perfect, on any device, with no extra storage. A memory, ready for the family group chat.

SEGMENT 6 — SEARCH AND OUTRO (~30s)
Months later, you'll want to find this photo again. Type wedding, or family, or child, and the vault searches every tag and caption instantly. That is the whole idea: an A I memory vault where nothing is ever lost. Everything you just saw runs on Cloudinary: uploads, A I tagging, restoration, enhancement, and cards.
